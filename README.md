# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 13, 2026
**Last Updated:** May 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Switchback Digital LLC** ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects personal information from clients, website visitors, and SMS subscribers. By using our services or opting in to our SMS messaging program, you agree to the practices described in this Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to deliver our services and operate our SMS messaging program. This may include:

- **Contact information:** name, email address, mailing address
- **Mobile information:** mobile phone number, mobile carrier, and SMS opt-in records (including the date, time, source, and method of consent)
- **Account and service information:** records of services requested, support inquiries, and communications with us
- **Message data:** delivery status, timestamps, and metadata associated with SMS messages sent through our Program
- **Website data:** standard log data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited when you visit our website

We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- To provide and operate our services
- To send the SMS alerts, notifications, and service-related messages you have opted in to receive
- To respond to support requests and client communications
- To maintain records of consent and opt-out as required by applicable law and carrier policies
- To improve, secure, and troubleshoot our services
- To comply with legal obligations

We do **not** use your information for marketing or promotional purposes without your separate, explicit consent.

---

## 3. SMS / Mobile Information

**Mobile information (including phone numbers and SMS opt-in data) will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.**

The only parties with whom mobile information may be shared are subprocessors that directly support the operation of the SMS service (for example, our SMS provider Twilio Inc., which transmits messages on our behalf). These subprocessors are bound by contractual confidentiality and data protection obligations and may use the information only to provide services to us.

This restriction applies to all mobile information collected through our SMS program and is not waived by any other provision of this Policy.

---

## 4. How We Share Information

Outside of the SMS-specific restriction in Section 3, we may share other personal information only in the following limited circumstances:

- **Service providers and subprocessors** who help us operate our business (such as hosting providers, email providers, payment processors, and our SMS provider), under appropriate confidentiality and data protection terms
- **Legal and safety reasons**, where disclosure is required to comply with applicable law, legal process, or a lawful government request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Switchback Digital LLC, our clients, or others
- **Business transfers**, in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, in which case we will take reasonable steps to ensure that the recipient honors the commitments in this Policy

We do not sell personal information.

---

## 5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as needed to provide our services, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. SMS opt-in and opt-out records are retained for the period required by applicable law and carrier policies, even after you unsubscribe from the Program.

## 6. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

- Request access to the personal information we hold about you
- Request correction or deletion of your personal information
- Withdraw consent to SMS messaging at any time by replying **STOP** to any message, or by contacting us
- Opt out of certain uses of your information

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information in Section 10. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.
