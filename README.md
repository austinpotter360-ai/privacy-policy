# SMS Messaging Policy

**Effective Date:** May 13, 2026
**Last Updated:** May 13, 2026

This SMS Messaging Policy describes how **Switchback Digital LLC** ("we," "us," or "our") operates its SMS messaging program. By providing your mobile phone number and opting in, you agree to the terms below.

---

## 1. Program Description

Switchback Digital LLC uses SMS to send transactional and service-related messages to clients who have explicitly opted in. Examples of messages you may receive include:

- Account and service alerts
- Appointment confirmations and reminders
- Status updates related to services you have requested
- Security notifications relevant to your account
- Customer support replies

We do **not** send marketing or promotional messages unless you have separately and explicitly opted in to receive them.

---

## 2. How to Opt In (Consent)

You may opt in to receive SMS messages from us through one of the following methods:

- Submitting your mobile phone number through a web form on **[your website URL]** with a clear, affirmative checkbox indicating your consent to receive SMS messages
- Providing your number in writing (email, signed agreement, or service intake form) and confirming consent to receive SMS
- Replying with a keyword such as `START` or `YES` to a verification message from our number

By opting in, you confirm that you are the subscriber of the mobile number provided or are authorized to provide it, and you consent to receive SMS messages from us at that number.

## 3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your account activity and the services you use. You may receive messages only when an event relevant to you occurs (for example, an alert, a status change, or a reply to a request you initiated).

---

## 4. Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan. Switchback Digital LLC does not charge for SMS messages, but standard carrier rates from your wireless provider will apply.

---

## 5. How to Opt Out (STOP)

You can cancel the SMS service at any time. Reply `STOP` to any message you receive from us. After you send `STOP`, we will send you a single confirmation message acknowledging that you have been unsubscribed, and you will not receive additional SMS messages from us unless you opt in again.

You may also request to be unsubscribed by emailing **[your support email]**.
